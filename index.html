What We Do

Our Services

Dryer Vent Cleaning

A full inspection, airflow test, and deep cleaning. Using a drill-attachment rotary brush and high-powered shop vac, we remove lint and debris from every inch of the vent — no shortcuts.

Most Popular Service

Duct Replacement

We replace flammable foil or plastic hoses with fire-rated DryerFlex duct, and repair disconnected or leaking joints inside walls or attics — bringing your system up to code.

Repair & Upgrade

Exterior Cap & Guard

We replace broken or bird-prone vent covers with high-efficiency DryerJack caps, plus wall vent upgrades designed to enhance airflow and withstand Bay Area coastal conditions.

Protection & Performance

MagVent Technology

Upgrade your dryer connection with MagVent — magnetic couplings that create a secure, efficient, low-maintenance seal with enhanced airflow and easier future cleanings.

Booster Fan Installation

For long vent runs or multi-floor buildings, a booster fan maintains strong airflow throughout the system — reducing lint buildup and fire risk in larger homes or multi-unit buildings.

About Us

A Family You Can
Trust

Dryer Vent Specialists is a family-owned, veteran-operated dryer vent cleaning company dedicated to the safety and efficiency of homes across San Mateo, CA and the greater San Francisco Bay Area. We specialize in residential dryer vent cleaning, vent repair, duct replacement, and booster fan installation.

Owned and operated by father and son Michael and Raymond Redman, we pride ourselves on delivering exceptional dryer vent service rooted in family values. Michael, a proud U.S. Veteran, brings discipline, integrity, and a commitment to excellence that defines every aspect of our work.
