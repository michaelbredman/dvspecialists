Got Questions?

South San Francisco FAQs

How often should I have my dryer vent cleaned? +
For most households, we recommend a professional cleaning once every year. However, if you have a large family, pets, or a dryer vent run longer than 15 feet, you may require service every 6 months to maintain safety and efficiency.
Can a clogged dryer vent really cause a fire? +
Yes — lint is highly combustible and dryer fires are one of the most common home fire hazards. The U.S. Fire Administration reports over 15,000 dryer fires annually, and the leading cause is failure to clean vents. Regular cleaning eliminates this risk at the source.
Do you offer dryer vent repair or just cleaning? +
We offer the full range: cleaning, repair, and replacement. If we find damaged duct, disconnected joints, or non-compliant flexible hose during a cleaning visit, we can repair or replace it on the same appointment in most cases.
How long does a typical service appointment take? +
Most standard dryer vent cleanings in South San Francisco take 45–60 minutes. More complex jobs — longer vent runs, roof exits, or duct replacements — may take 1.5–2 hours. We'll give you a time estimate when you book.
Will the cleaning process make a mess in my laundry room? +
No. We use a high-powered shop vacuum connected directly to the vent system throughout the cleaning process, so lint and debris are captured rather than dispersed. We leave your laundry room as clean as we found it.
Do you service condos and apartment buildings in South SF? +
Absolutely. South San Francisco has a high concentration of multi-unit housing, and we regularly service condos, apartment complexes, and HOA communities. We provide full documentation for property managers and HOA boards after every visit.
