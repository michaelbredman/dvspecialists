OUR DRYER VENT
SERVICES

From routine cleaning to full system upgrades, we keep your dryer running safely and efficiently. Every service includes a complete inspection and airflow verification.

What We Do

Residential & Commercial

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Our most popular service. A full inspection, airflow test, and deep cleaning using a drill-attachment rotary brush and high-powered shop vac. We remove lint and debris from every inch of the vent — from the dryer connection to the exterior cap.

  • ✓ Complete vent system inspection
  • ✓ Rotary brush + high-powered vacuum
  • ✓ Before & after airflow verification
  • ✓ Before & after photos provided
Most Popular Service

Duct Repair & Replacement

We replace flammable foil or plastic hoses with fire-rated DryerFlex duct, and repair disconnected or leaking joints inside walls or attics — bringing your system up to code.

  • ✓ UL 2158A compliant duct
  • ✓ Replace hazardous foil accordion hoses
  • ✓ Repair disconnected wall joints
  • ✓ Bring system up to building code
Repair & Upgrade

Exterior Cap & Guard

We replace broken or bird-prone vent covers with high-efficiency DryerJack termination caps, plus wall vent upgrades designed to enhance airflow and withstand Bay Area coastal conditions.

  • ✓ DryerJack wide-mouth cap
  • ✓ Pest & bird guard installation
  • ✓ Improved exhaust airflow
  • ✓ Weather-resistant materials
Protection & Performance

MagVent Technology

Upgrade your dryer connection with MagVent — magnetic couplings that create a secure, efficient, low-maintenance seal with enhanced airflow. The magnets auto-align when you push your dryer back, eliminating crushed hoses and making future cleanings a breeze.

  • ✓ Magnetic auto-align connection
  • ✓ Eliminates crushed transition ducts
  • ✓ Easy dryer access for maintenance

Booster Fan Installation

For long vent runs or multi-floor buildings, a booster fan maintains strong airflow throughout the system — reducing lint buildup and fire risk. Essential for duct runs over 25 equivalent feet.

  • ✓ Inline fan for long duct runs
  • ✓ Ideal for multi-story or multi-unit
  • ✓ Reduces drying time significantly
