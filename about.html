Our Story

Built on Family Values

Dryer Vent Specialists was founded by Michael and Raymond Redman - a father-son team united by a simple mission: make homes safer. After discovering how many house fires are caused by clogged dryer vents (over 15,000 per year according to the U.S. Fire Administration), they knew they could make a real difference in their community.

Michael, a proud U.S. military veteran, brings the discipline, attention to detail, and commitment to excellence that defined his years of service. Raymond brings technical expertise and a drive to deliver the best possible customer experience. Together, they've built a company rooted in honesty, hard work, and genuine care for every customer.

Based in San Mateo, CA, we serve homeowners and multi-unit properties across the entire San Francisco Bay Area Peninsula - from San Francisco to Half Moon Bay and everywhere in between.
