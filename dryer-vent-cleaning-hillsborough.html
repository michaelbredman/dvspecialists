Warning Signs

Signs Your Dryer Vent Needs Cleaning

If you notice any of these symptoms, your vent is likely partially or fully clogged and should be professionally cleaned as soon as possible. Hillsborough's large estate homes - including properties in Carolands and Upper Hillsborough - often feature 30- to 50-foot vent runs and roof terminations that accumulate lint faster than standard installations.

💨

Clothes need multiple cycles to dry

In Hillsborough estates with long vent runs routed through multiple floors, restricted airflow traps moisture - forcing your dryer to run two or three cycles per load.

⚠️

Dryer is very hot to the touch

If the top or sides of your dryer feel excessively hot, heat is building up inside due to restricted exhaust - especially common in Hillsborough homes with long roof-exit vents.

🔥

Burning smell when dryer is running

This is a critical fire hazard - the U.S. Fire Administration links overheated lint to approximately 2,900 home fires per year. Stop using the dryer and call immediately.

🩸

Lint trap catches less lint than usual

In Hillsborough homes with extended vent systems, lint bypasses the trap and collects deep in the ductwork - sometimes 40+ feet from the dryer. Professional equipment is needed to clear it.

💧

Laundry room feels humid or warm

A clogged vent forces hot, moist air back into your laundry room. In Hillsborough's larger homes, this may go unnoticed initially but can lead to mold in interior walls.

👁

Visible lint around exterior vent

Many Hillsborough homes have roof-exit vents that are difficult to inspect from the ground. If your dryer performance has declined, a professional inspection can verify the vent termination.

Hillsborough Dryer Vent Cleaning FAQ

How often should Hillsborough homeowners schedule dryer vent cleaning? +
The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) recommends professional dryer vent cleaning at least once per year. Hillsborough homes - many of which are large estates with laundry rooms far from exterior walls - often have dryer vent runs of 30 feet or more, with multiple elbows. These longer systems accumulate lint faster and should be cleaned every 6 months. Homes with multiple dryers, live-in staff doing frequent laundry, or households with pets should also clean more frequently.
Why is dryer vent cleaning especially important for large Hillsborough homes? +
Hillsborough is known for its large, estate-style properties - many built between the 1920s and 1960s - where laundry rooms may be located in basements, interior rooms, or second floors far from an exterior wall. This means dryer vent ductwork often runs 30 to 50 feet through walls, floors, and ceilings before reaching the outside. The U.S. Fire Administration reports that longer vent runs accumulate lint faster and restrict airflow more quickly, significantly increasing both fire risk and energy waste. Annual professional cleaning by a CDET-certified technician is essential for these configurations.

Yes. Many Hillsborough homes have dryer vents that exit through the roof rather than a side wall, which is common in hillside properties and homes where the laundry room is on an interior floor. What is CDET certification and why does it matter for Hillsborough dryer vent service? +
CDET stands for Certified Dryer Exhaust Technician, a professional credential issued by the Indoor Air Quality Association (IAQA). CDET-certified technicians are trained in NFPA fire code, duct material standards, UL 2158A transition duct requirements, and dryer exhaust system safety. Given the complexity of dryer vent systems in larger Hillsborough homes, CDET certification ensures the technician can properly assess extended runs, multi-bend configurations, and code compliance.
