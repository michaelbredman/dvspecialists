The Problem

Your Foil Duct Is a Fire Hazard

The flexible foil or plastic accordion duct connecting your dryer to the wall is the #1 source of dryer fires. It collapses behind the dryer, traps lint, and isn't rated for dryer exhaust temperatures. UL 2158A is the safety standard for transition ducts that are actually engineered for the job.
