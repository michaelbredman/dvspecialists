The Problem

What's Going On Behind Your Dryer?

⚠️ Fire Hazard

The Crush Point Is the #1 Danger Zone

A big problem faced in every laundry room is the crush point. Dryers naturally vibrate during use, or get pushed back against the wall when loading. A transition hose that's not built strong — like every duct that was available before DryerFlex — collapses under this pressure.

When the duct crushes, it restricts airflow and actually accelerates lint accumulation. Lint is extremely flammable. Combined with the high heat of dryer exhaust, a crushed duct creates a ticking fire hazard behind your dryer that you can't even see.

DryerFlex solves this. Its aluminum ribbon construction maintains a full 4" diameter even when compressed or bent, eliminating the crush point entirely and keeping airflow at maximum.
