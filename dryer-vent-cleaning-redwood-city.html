Redwood City Dryer Vent Cleaning FAQ

How often should Redwood City homeowners clean their dryer vents? +
The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) recommends professional dryer vent cleaning at least once per year. Redwood City homes — especially in neighborhoods like Woodside Plaza, Redwood Oaks, Palm Park, and Stambaugh-Heller — include a mix of mid-century single-family homes and newer multi-story construction. Older homes often have longer vent runs through walls and crawl spaces that trap lint faster. Households with 4 or more residents, pets, or vent runs exceeding 25 feet should schedule cleaning every 6 months.

What causes dryer fires and how common are they? +
According to the U.S. Fire Administration, approximately 2,900 home dryer fires occur each year in the United States, and lint buildup in the dryer exhaust vent is the leading cause. Lint is highly flammable and accumulates with every load of laundry. When airflow is restricted, the dryer overheats and the trapped lint can ignite. Professional cleaning removes this buildup, restores proper airflow, and is one of the most effective fire prevention measures for any home. The Redwood City Fire Department, like most Bay Area departments, recommends annual dryer vent inspection and cleaning.
Does Dryer Vent Specialists serve apartments and multi-unit buildings in Redwood City? +
Yes. Dryer Vent Specialists provides dryer vent cleaning for single-family homes, condominiums, apartment complexes, and commercial laundry facilities throughout Redwood City. Multi-unit properties — including buildings in downtown Redwood City, along Veterans Boulevard, and in the Redwood Shores area — often have shared or extended vent systems requiring specialized equipment. Volume pricing is available for properties with 4 or more units.
Will dryer vent cleaning reduce my energy bill? +
Yes. The U.S. Department of Energy reports that a clogged dryer vent can increase energy usage by 30% or more per drying cycle. For a typical Redwood City household running 4 to 5 loads per week, that adds approximately $15 to $25 per month to your PG&E bill. Professional cleaning restores full airflow, allowing the dryer to operate at peak efficiency. Most homeowners find that annual cleaning pays for itself in energy savings within a few months.
